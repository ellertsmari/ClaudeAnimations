## Intent

Recruitment video for an action research project run by two web-development teachers, Smári and Jakub (Vefskólinn, Tækniskólinn), this winter. They are looking for an ambitious teacher — not necessarily a programmer — who wants software that Canvas cannot do: a game for their students, a special way of assessing, or another innovative tool. Smári and Jakub plan and build the first version with the teacher, then teach the teacher to keep changing and improving it with AI, observe, reflect, and improve the support for this or the next teacher. Hypothesis: any teacher can create sophisticated software that fits their own group. Ties to the government report "Gervigreind til góðs" (2026): teachers first, training tied to real work.

Tone: exciting, inviting, playful, low threshold. Make it desirable so several teachers want to join.

Chosen concept (pitch 4, "Player 2 wanted"): a retro pixel arcade game. Smári and Jakub are Player 1 looking for a co-op Player 2. The AR cycles are game levels — Level 1: Hugmyndin, Level 2: Saman, Level 3: Þú við stýrið — and the teacher "levels up" from idea-owner to developer. Opens on a blinking "PLAYER 2 — ÝTTU Á START", closes on "INSERT HUGMYND". Different style from the user's previous watercolor video (the user wants to see the range of what is possible).
