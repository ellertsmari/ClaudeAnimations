AFREK OPNAÐ · VERÐLAUN

Silkscreen, small labels.

  • No glowing brains, robots or circuit clichés.
  • No smooth vector art or soft UI cards: pixels only.
  • No slideshow (every beat a fresh card) and no screensaver motion.
  • No promises of money, time off, deadlines or forms.

STILL OPEN · PLEASE CHECK

  1. Screen 03 says you both teach web development (“Vefþróun · Vefskólinn”) and built your own teaching systems. Is that right for Jakub?
  2. “Kennslukerfi #1 / #2” stand for your two LMSs. Real names instead?
  3. The pixel characters are generic, not portraits. Fine, or do you want a detail each (glasses, beard, hair colour)?
  4. Gunnar says “borð” for level; the screen shows LEVEL 1/2/3.
