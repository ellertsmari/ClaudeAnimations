THE STYLE GUIDE, APPLIED
  • Flat poster fields with 2–5 colours per shot, from black, cream, orange, blood red, electric blue and acid green.
  • Lopsided characters: mismatched oversized eyes, sharp teeth, jagged hair, rough outlines that boil.
  • Animated on twos (12 drawings/s at 24 fps), with snap pose changes, hard jump cuts and extreme close-ups.
  • One- or two-frame flash interruptions (inverted, silhouette or red impact frames), at most two per scene and never strobing.
